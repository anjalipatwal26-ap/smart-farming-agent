CROP SEASONS IN INDIA:
Kharif crops (June-October): Rice, Maize, Cotton, Soybean, Groundnut, Bajra, Jowar, Arhar, Moong, Urad, Sesame, Jute
Rabi crops (October-March): Wheat, Mustard, Gram, Peas, Barley, Linseed, Fenugreek, Coriander, Cumin
Zaid crops (March-June): Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Bitter gourd, Pumpkin, Moong, Urad

CROP SOWING CALENDAR:
Rice: Sow June-July, Harvest October-November. Requires flooded fields initially.
Wheat: Sow October-November, Harvest March-April. Best temp 10-25C.
Maize: Sow June-July, Harvest September-October. Needs good drainage.
Cotton: Sow April-May, Harvest October-December. Requires warm climate.
Mustard: Sow October, Harvest February-March. Drought tolerant.
Sugarcane: Sow February-March, Harvest December-February. 12-18 month crop.
Potato: Sow October-November, Harvest January-February. Cool climate needed.
Tomato: Sow June-July or November-December. Harvest after 60-70 days.
Onion: Sow November-December, Harvest March-May.
Groundnut: Sow June-July, Harvest October-November.
Soybean: Sow June-July, Harvest October. High protein crop.
Arhar/Tur Dal: Sow June-July, Harvest December-January. Drought tolerant.

SOIL TYPES AND SUITABLE CROPS:
Alluvial soil (Indo-Gangetic Plains - UP, Punjab, Haryana, Bihar):
- Best for: Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane, Cotton, Maize, Vegetables
- pH: 6.5-7.5, Very fertile, high nutrients
- Tip: Maintain organic matter, avoid waterlogging

Black soil / Regur soil (Maharashtra, MP, Gujarat, AP):
- Best for: Cotton, Soybean, Jowar, Wheat, Sunflower, Citrus
- pH: 7.5-8.5, High water retention, self-ploughing
- Tip: Avoid over-irrigation, good for rainfed farming

Red soil (AP, Tamil Nadu, Odisha, Jharkhand):
- Best for: Groundnut, Millets, Tobacco, Fruits, Vegetables
- pH: 5.5-7.0, Low in nitrogen and phosphorus
- Tip: Add organic manure and phosphatic fertilizers

Sandy/Desert soil (Rajasthan, Gujarat):
- Best for: Bajra, Moth bean, Cluster bean, Guar, Sesame
- pH: 7.0-8.5, Low water retention, low fertility
- Tip: Drip irrigation essential, add organic matter

Laterite soil (Kerala, Karnataka, West Bengal hills):
- Best for: Tea, Coffee, Cashew, Rubber, Coconut
- pH: 4.5-6.0, Acidic, low in lime and nitrogen
- Tip: Add lime to reduce acidity, use green manure

Mountain soil (Himalayan states - HP, Uttarakhand, J&K):
- Best for: Apple, Pear, Apricot, Walnut, Tea, Potato
- pH: 5.0-6.5, Rich in humus
- Tip: Terracing essential to prevent erosion

FERTILIZER GUIDE:
NPK meaning: N=Nitrogen (leaf/stem growth), P=Phosphorus (root/flower), K=Potassium (fruit/grain quality)

Wheat fertilizer:
- Apply 120kg N + 60kg P + 40kg K per hectare
- Split N: 50% basal + 25% at crown root + 25% at jointing
- Use Urea for N, DAP for P, MOP for K
- Apply zinc sulfate 25kg/ha if zinc deficient

Rice fertilizer:
- Apply 120kg N + 60kg P + 60kg K per hectare
- Apply N in 3 splits: basal, tillering, panicle initiation
- Use Urea, DAP, MOP
- Apply zinc sulfate 25kg/ha in zinc deficient soils

Cotton fertilizer:
- Apply 150kg N + 60kg P + 60kg K per hectare
- Apply boron 1kg/ha to prevent boll shedding
- Use FYM 10 tonnes/ha before sowing

Tomato fertilizer:
- Apply 100kg N + 60kg P + 80kg K per hectare
- Use calcium nitrate to prevent blossom end rot
- Foliar spray micronutrients every 15 days

Potato fertilizer:
- Apply 150kg N + 100kg P + 150kg K per hectare
- High potassium improves tuber quality

Organic options:
- FYM (Farm Yard Manure): 10-15 tonnes/ha, apply 3-4 weeks before sowing
- Vermicompost: 5 tonnes/ha, excellent for all crops
- Neem cake: 200kg/ha, acts as fertilizer + pest repellent
- Green manure: Dhaincha/Sunhemp, incorporate before flowering

PEST AND DISEASE CONTROL:
Rice pests:
- Stem borer: Yellow/white deadheart. Use Chlorpyrifos 20EC @ 2.5ml/L or Cartap hydrochloride
- Brown planthopper: Yellowing, hopper burn. Use Imidacloprid 17.8SL @ 0.3ml/L
- Leaf folder: Folded leaves, white streaks. Use Monocrotophos 36SL @ 1.5ml/L
- Blast disease: Diamond shaped lesions. Use Tricyclazole 75WP @ 0.6g/L
- Sheath blight: Brown lesions on sheath. Use Hexaconazole 5EC @ 2ml/L

Wheat pests:
- Aphids: Curled leaves, sticky honeydew. Use Dimethoate 30EC @ 1ml/L or neem oil
- Rust (yellow/brown/black): Colored pustules on leaves. Use Propiconazole 25EC @ 1ml/L
- Loose smut: Black powdery head. Use seed treatment with Carboxin 75WP
- Termites: Wilting plants. Use Chlorpyrifos in irrigation water

Cotton pests:
- Bollworm (American/Spotted/Pink): Damaged bolls. Use Bt spray, Spinosad, or Emamectin
- Whitefly: Yellowing, sticky leaves, virus vector. Use Neem oil + Yellow sticky traps
- Thrips: Silver streaks on leaves. Use Fipronil 5SC @ 1.5ml/L
- Root rot: Wilting, brown roots. Seed treatment with Trichoderma

Vegetable pests:
- Aphids on vegetables: Neem oil 5ml/L + soap solution
- Fruit borer on tomato/brinjal: Spinosad 45SC or pheromone traps
- Downy mildew: Blue-grey fungal growth. Use Metalaxyl + Mancozeb
- Early blight on tomato: Brown concentric rings. Use Mancozeb 75WP @ 2g/L

General organic pest control:
- Neem oil spray: 5ml/L water, effective for soft-bodied insects
- Neem cake soil application: Repels soil pests
- Yellow sticky traps: For whitefly, thrips, aphids
- Pheromone traps: For bollworm, fruit borer
- Trichogramma cards: Biological control for stem borers
- Beauveria bassiana: Fungal biocontrol for many pests

IRRIGATION GUIDE:
Drip irrigation:
- Best for: Fruits, vegetables, sugarcane, cotton
- Water saving: 40-50% compared to flood irrigation
- Fertigation possible (apply fertilizer through drip)

Sprinkler irrigation:
- Best for: Wheat, groundnut, vegetables, nurseries
- Water saving: 25-30% compared to flood irrigation
- Not suitable for rice

Flood irrigation:
- Used for: Rice (paddy), sugarcane
- Requires: Level land, bunds around fields

Critical irrigation stages:
Wheat: Crown root initiation (21 days), Tillering (45 days), Jointing (65 days), Flowering (90 days), Grain filling (105 days)
Rice: Transplanting, Tillering, Panicle initiation, Flowering, Grain filling
Maize: Knee height, Tasseling, Silking, Grain filling
Cotton: Squaring, Flowering, Boll development

Water stress signs:
- Leaf rolling/wilting in morning: Immediate irrigation needed
- Soil cracking: Severe water stress
- Bluish-green color change: Early water stress

WEATHER AND FARMING DECISIONS:
Temperature effects:
- Below 5°C: Frost risk. Cover nurseries, spray water on plants at night
- 5-15°C: Good for wheat, mustard, vegetables
- 15-25°C: Ideal for most crops
- 25-35°C: Good for rice, cotton, maize
- Above 40°C: Heat stress. Increase irrigation, mulching recommended
- Above 45°C: Severe stress, avoid field operations

Humidity effects:
- Below 40%: Drought stress, spider mites active
- 40-70%: Ideal range for most crops
- Above 70%: Fungal disease risk increases
- Above 85%: High risk of blast, blight, mildew diseases

Rain and farming:
- Rain forecast within 24 hours: Do NOT spray pesticides
- Heavy rain forecast: Postpone fertilizer application
- After rain: Apply fungicide preventively in humid conditions
- Dry spell >2 weeks: Start irrigation immediately

Wind effects:
- High winds: Avoid spraying pesticides
- Hot dry winds (Loo): Increase irrigation frequency
- Coastal winds: Salt spray can damage crops near coast

ORGANIC FARMING GUIDE:
Principles:
- No synthetic fertilizers or pesticides
- Use of compost, FYM, vermicompost
- Crop rotation to maintain soil health
- Intercropping for natural pest control

Organic certification:
- PGS India: Participatory Guarantee System
- Third party: IMO, ECOCERT, OneCert
- Process takes 3 years for conversion

Organic inputs:
- Jeevamrit: Mix 200L water + 10kg desi cow dung + 10L desi cow urine + 2kg jaggery + 2kg gram flour + handful soil. Ferment 48 hours. Apply 200L/acre monthly
- Panchagavya: Cow dung + urine + milk + curd + ghee. Spray 3% solution
- Beejamrit: Seed treatment before sowing with cow dung solution

FARM MANAGEMENT TIPS:
Crop rotation benefits:
- Legume after cereal: Fixes nitrogen, reduces fertilizer need
- Example: Rice-Wheat-Moong rotation in Punjab/UP
- Reduces pest and disease buildup

Intercropping benefits:
- Maize + Moong: Increases income per acre
- Sugarcane + Wheat: Utilizes space efficiently
- Arhar + Soybean: Natural pest management

Post harvest management:
- Dry produce to safe moisture levels before storage
- Wheat: 12% moisture for safe storage
- Use hermetic bags or metal bins to prevent weevils
- Apply phosphine tablets in sealed storage

Soil health improvement:
- Add organic matter every season
- Avoid burning crop residue - incorporate it
- Subsoil ploughing every 3-4 years
- Green manure crops in fallow period
